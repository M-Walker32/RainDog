About Rain Dog Wines

Rain Dog is some of the finest wine in the high desert. Low sulfites, low intervention - the way God intended. This Snake River Valley Viognier has distinct aromatics and vibrant acidity due to the vineyards significant diurnal temperature shifts. It’s fermented in stainless steel to keep it crisp and refreshing. All that to say, it’s good wine for drinkin’. 

Inside a broken clock, splashing the wine with all the Rain Dogs. Taxi, we'd rather walk. Huddle a doorway with the Rain Dogs for I am a Rain Dog, too.
